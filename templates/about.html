How it works

Two connected decision layers

Layer 1

Machine-learning risk estimation

The model receives step, transaction type, amount, origin balance and destination balance. It creates the same leakage-safe features used during training and returns a calibrated risk estimate.

Layer 2

Q-learning action selection

The policy combines fraud-risk state, amount state and time state. It compares Q-values for Approve, Additional Authentication, Manual Review and Block.

Safety

Coverage checks and guardrails

Sparse states use a transparent fallback rule. High-risk guardrails prevent unsafe approval when Q-table evidence is insufficient or noisy.

Leakage-safe inputs

Only information known before completion

The application deliberately excludes new origin and destination balances. Those values describe what happened after a transaction and would make a real-time decision unrealistically easy.
